# Late fee policy

A late fee of 10 dollars is posted on day 15 after the due date. The fee is charged once per invoice, not once per line. Accounts that are already in a written hardship arrangement are not charged the fee for the invoices covered by that arrangement. The fee is a posted charge and follows the ordinary refund review if it was applied in error. Paying the overdue balance on day 14 avoids the fee.
