# International roaming

International roaming in Canada and Mexico is included on the Unlimited Talk plan. It uses the same premium data allowance as domestic use. Travel outside Canada and Mexico is billed at 10 dollars per day when the line registers on a partner network, and the customer can turn daily travel off in the app before the trip. Prepaid 30 does not include Canada or Mexico. Roaming talk is unlimited only while the daily travel pass or the included region applies.
