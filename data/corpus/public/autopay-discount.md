# Autopay discount

Autopay gives a 5 dollar monthly discount. The discount applies to postpaid fiber and postpaid mobile invoices. It does not apply to prepaid plans, one-time installation, or equipment charges. The payment method on file must succeed on the due date. If the payment fails, the discount is removed for that invoice and the ordinary balance remains due.
