# Replacing a SIM

A customer can freeze a missing SIM from the Northline app before a replacement arrives. Freezing stops calls, texts, and data on that line. The replacement SIM is 5 dollars by mail or 0 dollars when picked up during a scheduled support chat. The customer enters the new SIM number in the app to move the line. Until the new SIM is activated, the freeze stays on.
