# Privacy summary

Northline keeps precise location history for 14 days and then deletes it. Account holders can download the remaining history from the privacy page in the app. Call detail records are kept for 18 months because invoices reference them. This page is a customer summary. It does not describe internal fraud review. Marketing email can be turned off without closing the account.
