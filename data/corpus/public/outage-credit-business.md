# Outage credit for small business customers

A continuous outage of 8 hours or more on a small-business customer account is handled as half a day of the business recurring charge. This is not the residential customer rule and it is not the residential agent rule.

## How the request is opened

A small-business customer outage credit starts from the business banner in the account app. The qualifying continuous gap is 8 hours, which is not the figure on the neighboring page. The size of the credit is half a day of the business recurring charge. Planned work that was announced at least 24 hours ahead does not qualify on this page. The business account holder requests it. Residential rules are not used on a business account. Cash is not offered on this page. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for small-business customer outage credit of half a day of the business recurring charge. They compare small-business customer outage credit of half a day of the business recurring charge with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a small-business customer outage credit of half a day of the business recurring charge note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. ## What gets confused with the neighboring page

The neighboring page is the residential customer outage page. That page uses a different size (one full day after 12 hours) and a different audience. Quoting one full day after 12 hours on a small-business customer note is a misread. This page's size stays half a day of the business recurring charge even when the caller asks for the other figure. ## What does not qualify

A gap shorter than the figure on this page does not qualify. A single television app, a handset on wireless, or a gateway that was unplugged does not qualify. The credit is service days or a stated fraction of a day, posted on a later invoice. It is not a refund of a payment, and it is not the the residential customer outage page rule.
