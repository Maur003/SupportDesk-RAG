# Paperless billing

Paperless billing is required to receive the autopay discount. A paper invoice is 3 dollars each month when the customer opts in to mail. The paper invoice is sent to the service address on the account. Changing the billing email does not by itself turn paper invoices back on. Customers can switch to paperless in the app and the next invoice follows the new choice.
