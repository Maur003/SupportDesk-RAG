# Missed visit fee

The missed visit fee is 25 dollars. This page states the charge a household sees. It does not state who may waive it.

## What the household is billed

The billed figure is 25 dollars. A neighboring fee page uses a different figure and a different name. Do not subtract this figure from a plan price. It is its own line.
