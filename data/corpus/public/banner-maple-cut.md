# Maple Cut service banner

When the Maple Cut banner MC-21 is up, the household checks the power light on the optical terminal before opening a ticket. A dark power light is a local problem. A lit power light with no service is banner MC-21. The household quotes banner MC-21 and does not quote another spur's code.
