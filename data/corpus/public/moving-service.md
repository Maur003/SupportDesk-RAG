# Moving fiber service

A move of existing fiber service costs 40 dollars. The customer keeps the same plan and the same gateway if the new address is on the fiber network. The move is scheduled as an installation at the new address and a disconnect at the old address on the same day when both slots exist. If the new address is not on the network, the customer is told before any disconnect is ordered. The 40 dollar charge is posted after the new service is active.
