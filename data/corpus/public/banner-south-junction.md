# South Junction service banner

When the South Junction banner SJ-18 is up, the household checks the power light on the optical terminal before opening a ticket. A dark power light is a local problem. A lit power light with no service is banner SJ-18. The household quotes banner SJ-18 and does not quote another spur's code.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner SJ-18 is the South Junction code only. A neighboring spur uses a different code and a different first check. Quote SJ-18 when the app shows that banner.
