# Early termination fee

The early termination fee is 12 dollars for each month remaining on a term contract. Month-to-month service has no early termination fee. The fee is a posted charge on the final invoice. Customers who move to an address Northline cannot serve are not charged the fee when they disconnect within 30 days of the move request. The remaining months are counted from the disconnect date to the contract end date.
