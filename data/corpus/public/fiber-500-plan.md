# Fiber 500 residential plan

The Fiber 500 plan costs 79 dollars per month. It includes unlimited data at speeds up to 500 Mbps download and 500 Mbps upload on the Northline wireline network. A residential gateway is included for the length of the subscription. The plan is month to month unless the customer signs a separate term agreement. Taxes are billed in addition to the 79 dollar charge. The plan does not include mobile service.
