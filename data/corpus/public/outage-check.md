# What to do during an outage

## Check status first

Customers should open the Northline app and look at the outage banner for their service address before restarting equipment. If the banner says a neighborhood outage is open, the customer does not need to reboot the gateway.

## If no banner is posted

Unplug the gateway, wait 60 seconds, and plug it back in. If the optical light stays red after 10 minutes, the customer should start a chat and quote the gateway serial number from the sticker.
