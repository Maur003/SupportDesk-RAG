# Voicemail

The voicemail greeting can be up to 60 seconds long. A missed call goes to voicemail after 25 seconds of ringing. Messages are stored for 30 days and then deleted. The customer sets the voicemail PIN in the app. Resetting that PIN does not reset the account PIN used for support.
