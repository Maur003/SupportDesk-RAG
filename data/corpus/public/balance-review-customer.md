# Asking for a balance review

A household asks for a balance review from the billing page in the app. This page does not say who may erase a balance, and it does not give a desk code.

## What an agent may say

Frontline staff open a billing ticket and stop. They do not promise that a balance will be erased. Dollar lines live on a finance page that is not this one. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for customer balance review. They compare customer balance review with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a customer balance review note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months.
