# Fiber 2000 residential plan

The Fiber 2000 plan costs 129 dollars per month. It is a residential wireline tier and is not Fiber 1000. Download and upload peaks are 2000 Mbps and 2000 Mbps. One static address is included on this tier and on no slower tier.
