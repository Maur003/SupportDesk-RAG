# When a reviewed refund shows up

A reviewed refund of a posted payment shows on the account within 7 business days. The clock starts when the review is finished, not when the household asked. This page does not say who is allowed to approve the review.

## What a refund is not

Seven business days is a posting clock for the household. It is not an approval cap. A service credit reduces a later invoice and is described on a different page. The neighboring page is the finance same-day posting page. The ledger page describes the day the approval is recorded, which is a different clock from these 7 business days. Mixing the two pages produces the wrong answer. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for 7 business day refund posting.
