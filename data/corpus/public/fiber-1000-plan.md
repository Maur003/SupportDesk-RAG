# Fiber 1000 residential plan

The Fiber 1000 plan costs 99 dollars per month. Download and upload speeds are up to 1000 Mbps on fiber. The same residential gateway used on Fiber 500 is included. Customers who already have Fiber 500 can upgrade without a truck roll when the optical terminal supports it. The 99 dollar price is the recurring service charge and does not include taxes or one-time installation.
