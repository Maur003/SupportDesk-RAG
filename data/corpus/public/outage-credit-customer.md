# Outage credit for residential customers

A continuous outage of 12 hours or more qualifies a residential customer to request a one-day service credit. The customer requests it from the outage banner or from a billing ticket. The credit is one day of the plan's recurring charge, not a cash payment. Planned maintenance that was announced at least 24 hours ahead does not qualify. This page does not state what an agent may apply without further review.

## What does not qualify

A gap shorter than the figure on this page does not qualify. A single television app, a handset on wireless, or a gateway that was unplugged does not qualify. The credit is service days or a stated fraction of a day, posted on a later invoice. It is not a refund of a payment, and it is not the the agent outage-credit page rule.
