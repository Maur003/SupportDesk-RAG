# Prepaid 30 mobile plan

The Prepaid 30 plan expires 30 days after activation. It includes 10 gigabytes of mobile data, unlimited talk, and unlimited text. The price is 30 dollars paid before the line is used. There is no autopay discount on prepaid. A customer can renew from the Northline app on the day the plan expires. Unused data is not carried into the next 30 days.
