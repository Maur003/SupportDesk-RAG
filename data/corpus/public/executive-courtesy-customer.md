# Asking the executive office for a courtesy adjustment

A household may write to the executive office. This page does not say who must sign, and it does not give a desk code or a dollar line.

## What an agent may say

Frontline staff can open a ticket and mark that the caller asked for the executive office. They cannot create the credit. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for customer executive request. They compare customer executive request with the neighboring page before they quote it.
