# Lake Switch service banner

When the Lake Switch banner LS-11 is up, the household checks the power light on the optical terminal before opening a ticket. A dark power light is a local problem. A lit power light with no service is banner LS-11. The household quotes banner LS-11 and does not quote another spur's code.
