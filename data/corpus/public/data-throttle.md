# Mobile speed after the allowance

After the premium data allowance, mobile speeds are reduced to 2 Mbps. The line is not disconnected. Video may buffer. The allowance resets on the billing date for postpaid lines and on the renewal date for prepaid lines. A customer can add a 5 gigabyte top-up for 10 dollars from the app. The top-up is premium data and is used before the reduced speed applies again.
