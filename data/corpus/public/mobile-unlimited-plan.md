# Unlimited Talk mobile plan

The Unlimited Talk plan includes 50 gigabytes of premium mobile data. After that allowance, the line stays connected at a reduced speed. Talk and text inside the United States are unlimited. A single line is 65 dollars per month. Each added line is 30 dollars per month. The plan can be billed on the same invoice as fiber.
