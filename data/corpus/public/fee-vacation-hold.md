# Vacation hold

The vacation hold is 10 dollars per month while the hold lasts. This page states the charge a household sees. It does not state who may waive it.

## What the household is billed

The billed figure is 10 dollars per month while the hold lasts. A neighboring fee page uses a different figure and a different name. Do not subtract this figure from a plan price. It is its own line.
