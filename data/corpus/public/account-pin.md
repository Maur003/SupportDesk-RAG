# Account PIN reset

Account PIN resets require the last four digits of the account number and a one-time text code. The text is sent to the mobile line on the account, or to the contact number on a fiber-only account. An agent cannot read the PIN back to the customer. After three failed attempts the reset is locked for 30 minutes. The new PIN is chosen by the customer and is four digits.
