# Fiber 300 residential plan

The Fiber 300 plan costs 59 dollars per month. It is a residential wireline tier and is not Fiber 500. Download and upload peaks are 300 Mbps and 300 Mbps. No static address is included on this tier.

## Wired speed test

Fiber 300 is advertised at 300 Mbps toward the home and 300 Mbps toward the network. The desk measures that on a cable plugged into the gateway, not over the air. A result at or above 80 percent of 300 Mbps is treated as inside the home. A result below that line can become a plant ticket. Do not quote the Fiber 500 speed of 500 Mbps on a Fiber 300 ticket.
