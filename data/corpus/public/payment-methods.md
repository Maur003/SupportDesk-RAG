# Accepted payment methods

Northline accepts Visa, Mastercard, and ACH bank drafts. Debit cards that use those networks are accepted. Northline does not accept cash by mail. A returned ACH draft is treated as a failed payment and the autopay discount is removed for that invoice. The customer can replace the payment method in the app before the next due date.
