# Returning a gateway

A gateway not returned within 21 days is billed at 150 dollars. The 21 days start on the service disconnect date. The customer can drop the gateway at a Northline ship-back counter listed in the app, or use the prepaid mailer that arrives after disconnect. A damaged gateway is still accepted. The charge is removed when the warehouse scans the serial number.
