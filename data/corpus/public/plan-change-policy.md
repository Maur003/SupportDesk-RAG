# Plan changes

Plan changes requested before noon Eastern Time take effect the next billing date. A request at or after noon is dated the following day and then waits for that next billing date. Noon is 12:00 Eastern Time, including weekends.

# Proration rules

1. A customer may change plans once per billing period.
2. The new plan must be available at the service address.
3. Mobile line changes do not change the fiber plan.
4. Fiber changes do not reset the mobile data allowance.
5. A term contract remains in force after an upgrade.
6. A downgrade during a term contract can create an early termination review.
7. The review uses the early termination page and is not decided by this page.
8. Agents submit the change in the billing system before the cutoff.
9. Customers can submit the same change in the app.
10. A change submitted twice is processed once.
11. The second submission is marked duplicate and ignored.
12. Taxes are recomputed from the new recurring charge.
13. Promotional credits that name the old plan end on the change date.
14. Autopay stays on file across the change.
15. Paperless billing stays as it was.
16. The gateway is replaced only when the new plan requires a different model.
17. A technician is not scheduled for a same-model plan change.
18. Prepaid lines cannot be changed to postpaid on this form.
19. Postpaid to prepaid is a disconnect and a new activation.
20. The account PIN is required in the app before the change is accepted.
21. An agent uses the identity verification steps before submitting for the customer.
22. A pending installation blocks a plan change until the appointment is completed or canceled.
23. A neighborhood outage banner does not block a plan change.
24. The invoice shows the old charge and the new charge on separate lines when the change splits the month.
25. Split-month charges are prorated by the day.
26. The day of the change is billed at the new plan.
27. Credits from proration appear on the following invoice.
28. Customers who disagree with proration open a billing ticket.
29. That ticket does not reverse the plan change by itself.
30. Finance reviews proration disputes.
31. Agents quote this page and do not invent a different cutoff.

# Downgrades

If a customer downgrades Fiber 1000 to Fiber 500, the price difference is credited on the following invoice and is not returned as cash. The downgrade still has to be requested before noon Eastern Time to keep the timing rule above. The Fiber 500 recurring charge replaces the Fiber 1000 charge on the effective billing date.
