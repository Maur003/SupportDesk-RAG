# How a customer requests a refund

Customers request a refund from the billing page in the Northline app or by asking an agent to open a billing ticket. The request must name the invoice and the charge. Refunds of posted payments are reviewed by the billing team. This page does not list approval amounts. The customer is told the ticket number and can ask for status with that number. A refund is not the same as a service credit.

## What a refund is not

This page tells a household how to ask. It does not list approval amounts. A service credit reduces a later invoice and is described on a different page. The neighboring page is the finance refund matrix. That matrix is not on this page, and its desk code is not repeated here. Mixing the two pages produces the wrong answer. The household names the invoice and the charge in the app, or asks frontline staff to open a billing ticket. The invoice id and the charge line are required before anyone reviews the request.
