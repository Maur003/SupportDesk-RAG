# How a customer requests a refund

Customers request a refund from the billing page in the Northline app or by asking an agent to open a billing ticket. The request must name the invoice and the charge. Refunds of posted payments are reviewed by the billing team. This page does not list approval amounts. The customer is told the ticket number and can ask for status with that number. A refund is not the same as a service credit.
