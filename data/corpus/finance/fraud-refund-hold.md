# Refund holds on fraud flags

A refund or credit is held when the account has an open fraud flag. Only the fraud desk can clear the flag. Finance does not release the money while the flag is open, even if the amount is inside an analyst's limit. The customer is told that the payment is under review and is given the ticket number. The hold is not a write-off.
