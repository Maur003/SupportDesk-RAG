# Chargeback handling

A chargeback notice must be answered within 7 calendar days. Finance gathers the invoice, the payment authorization, and the service start date. Agents do not reply to the card network. If the chargeback is lost, the amount is posted back to the account and a finance analyst decides whether to pursue the balance. The 7 day clock starts when the notice arrives in the finance queue.
