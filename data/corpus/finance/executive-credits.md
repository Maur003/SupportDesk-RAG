# Executive courtesy credits

An executive courtesy credit above 150 dollars requires the finance director. Credits at or below 150 dollars can be approved by a finance analyst when an executive office forwarded the request in writing. The credit is still a service credit unless a refund was approved under the refund matrix. The forwarding note is stored on the ticket. Agents cannot create this credit type.
