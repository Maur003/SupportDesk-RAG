# Payment posted to the wrong account

Payment posted to the wrong account means a payment that landed on a different account number. The ledger rule is: move it the same day and do not treat it as a refund. This is not the refund matrix, the goodwill cap, or the write-off page.

## The ledger rule

Follow this rule only: move it the same day and do not treat it as a refund. The definition is a payment that landed on a different account number. Name the invoice and the payment id on the note. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for Payment posted to the wrong account: move it the same day and do not treat it as a refund.
