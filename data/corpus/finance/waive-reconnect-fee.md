# Waiving the reconnect fee

The ledger name for this line is reconnect fee, billed at 35 dollars. Finance may waive or cap it under this rule: one reconnect fee in a year. Frontline staff cannot waive it.

## Who may change the line

The finance rule is one reconnect fee in a year. That rule is not the rule on the household page, which only states 35 dollars. A waiver note names the invoice, the line, and the rule that was used. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for waive Reconnect fee (one reconnect fee in a year). They compare waive Reconnect fee (one reconnect fee in a year) with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a waive Reconnect fee (one reconnect fee in a year) note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. A later reader looks for waive Reconnect fee (one reconnect fee in a year) and does not copy the note onto another page.
