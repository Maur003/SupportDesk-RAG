# Waiving the missed visit fee

The ledger name for this line is missed visit fee, billed at 25 dollars. Finance may waive or cap it under this rule: when the technician arrived outside the window. Frontline staff credit it only when the window log shows the miss.

## Who may change the line

The finance rule is when the technician arrived outside the window. That rule is not the rule on the household page, which only states 25 dollars. A waiver note names the invoice, the line, and the rule that was used. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for waive Missed visit fee (when the technician arrived outside the window). They compare waive Missed visit fee (when the technician arrived outside the window) with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a waive Missed visit fee (when the technician arrived outside the window) note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. A later reader looks for waive Missed visit fee (when the technician arrived outside the window) and does not copy the note onto another page.
