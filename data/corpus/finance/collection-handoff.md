# Collection handoff

An account is handed to the outside collection desk after 120 days past due if it was not written off and no hardship arrangement is active. Finance sends the balance, the last invoice id, and the contact number. Agents stop outbound collection calls on the day of the handoff. A payment that arrives after handoff is still accepted and is reported to the desk the same day.
