# Waiving the paper bill fee

The ledger name for this line is paper bill fee, billed at 5 dollars each invoice. Finance may waive or cap it under this rule: a hardship arrangement only. Frontline staff cannot waive it.

## Who may change the line

The finance rule is a hardship arrangement only. That rule is not the rule on the household page, which only states 5 dollars each invoice. A waiver note names the invoice, the line, and the rule that was used. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for waive Paper bill fee (a hardship arrangement only). They compare waive Paper bill fee (a hardship arrangement only) with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a waive Paper bill fee (a hardship arrangement only) note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. A later reader looks for waive Paper bill fee (a hardship arrangement only) and does not copy the note onto another page.
