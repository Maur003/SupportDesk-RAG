# Waiving the residential deposit

The ledger name for this line is residential deposit, billed at 100 dollars. Finance may waive or cap it under this rule: after 12 invoices paid on time. Frontline staff cannot send the deposit back.

## Who may change the line

The finance rule is after 12 invoices paid on time. That rule is not the rule on the household page, which only states 100 dollars. A waiver note names the invoice, the line, and the rule that was used. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for waive Residential deposit (after 12 invoices paid on time). They compare waive Residential deposit (after 12 invoices paid on time) with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a waive Residential deposit (after 12 invoices paid on time) note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. A later reader looks for waive Residential deposit (after 12 invoices paid on time) and does not copy the note onto another page.
