# Waiving the call forward add-on

The ledger name for this line is call forward add-on, billed at 4 dollars. Finance may waive or cap it under this rule: prorated to the day it was removed. Frontline staff can remove it and cannot prorate it.

## Who may change the line

The finance rule is prorated to the day it was removed. That rule is not the rule on the household page, which only states 4 dollars. A waiver note names the invoice, the line, and the rule that was used. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for waive Call forward add-on (prorated to the day it was removed). They compare waive Call forward add-on (prorated to the day it was removed) with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a waive Call forward add-on (prorated to the day it was removed) note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. A later reader looks for waive Call forward add-on (prorated to the day it was removed) and does not copy the note onto another page.
