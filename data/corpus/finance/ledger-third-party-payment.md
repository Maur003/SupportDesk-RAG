# Payment from a third party

Payment from a third party means a payment that arrives from someone who is not the account holder. The ledger rule is: hold it until the account holder confirms it in the app. This is not the refund matrix, the goodwill cap, or the write-off page.

## The ledger rule

Follow this rule only: hold it until the account holder confirms it in the app. The definition is a payment that arrives from someone who is not the account holder.
