# Goodwill credit caps

A finance analyst may approve a goodwill credit up to 50 dollars without a second signature. A goodwill credit from 51 dollars through 150 dollars needs the finance director. Goodwill credits are service credits on a future invoice, not cash, unless a separate refund was also approved. Agents stop at their own outage-credit rule and send anything else here. The reason code on the credit must be goodwill, not outage, when no banner exists.
