# When the ledger posts an approved reversal

The ledger posts an approved reversal on the same business day the approval is recorded. That clock is not the 7 business days a household is told. This page does not replace the approval matrix.

## What a refund is not

Same-day posting is a ledger clock. It is not permission to approve. A service credit reduces a later invoice and is described on a different page. The neighboring page is the household 7 business day page. Quoting 7 business days on a ledger note, or quoting same-day posting to a household, is a misread. Mixing the two pages produces the wrong answer. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for same-business-day ledger posting. They compare same-business-day ledger posting with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a same-business-day ledger posting note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. ## Timing versus approval

Post on the same business day the approval is recorded. Do not wait 7 business days on the ledger. Timing and approval are different questions. A page that states a clock does not state who may approve, and a page that states approval does not promise a clock unless it says so.
