# Write-off policy

Accounts above 200 dollars past due for 90 days may be written off only by the finance director. Smaller balances can be written off by a finance analyst after two failed payment arrangements. A write-off does not erase fraud flags. The account is closed after the write-off posts. Finance does not ask agents to promise a write-off on a call.
