# Returned payment

Returned payment means a payment the bank sent back. The ledger rule is: the returned-payment line is 15 dollars and is not the late fee. This is not the refund matrix, the goodwill cap, or the write-off page.

## The ledger rule

Follow this rule only: the returned-payment line is 15 dollars and is not the late fee. The definition is a payment the bank sent back. Name the invoice and the payment id on the note.
