# Promo credit accounting

Promotional credits are recognized over the months they apply, not all in the month they are granted. A 15 dollar save for 6 months is 15 dollars on each of six invoices. Finance reverses the unearned months if the customer disconnects early. The reversal is not an early termination fee. The ledger code is PROMO-SAVE. Agents do not pick the ledger code.
