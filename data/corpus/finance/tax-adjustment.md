# Tax adjustments

A tax adjustment request is filed by the finance team, not by a store agent. The request names the invoice, the tax line, and the reason, such as a corrected jurisdiction after a move. Finance posts the adjustment on the next invoice. A customer who believes a tax is wrong is given a billing ticket, and the agent routes it to finance without estimating the new tax.
