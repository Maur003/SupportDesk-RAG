# Device installment settlement

A device installment plan can be settled early with no prepayment penalty. The payoff amount is the remaining principal and does not include future service charges. Finance posts the payoff as its own charge line. Agents can read the payoff amount from the billing system. They cannot forgive principal. Forgiveness follows the goodwill caps.
