# East Yard dispatch

For banner EY-22, dispatch Yard Night when optical loss stays above 2.8 dB for 35 minutes. Do not dispatch Yard Night for a single apartment under that line. The 2.8 dB line is East Yard only.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner EY-22 maps to East Yard. A ticket that names a different banner is a different spur and a different crew. Write EY-22, the dB reading, and the clock time before paging Yard Night. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for East Yard EY-22 at 2.8 dB. They compare East Yard EY-22 at 2.8 dB with the neighboring page before they quote it.
