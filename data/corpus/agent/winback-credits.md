# Win-back credits

A win-back credit of 20 dollars is available when a disconnected postpaid customer restarts service within 30 days. Agents apply it once. It does not stack with the retention save offer. Prepaid restarts do not qualify. The credit posts on the first new invoice. Agents record the old account number on the new account so the credit can be traced.
