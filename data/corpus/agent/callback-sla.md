# Callback SLA

The callback SLA for a waiting customer is 30 minutes during phone support hours. Agents offer a callback only while phone support is open, from 08:00 to 20:00 Eastern Time. A missed callback is placed again once. The second miss becomes a ticket with priority 3 unless the customer described a full outage. Agents do not promise a callback outside phone support hours.
