# Fraud flags for agents

Agents flag a ticket as suspected fraud when a caller asks to move a line to a new SIM and cannot pass identity verification. The line stays frozen. Agents do not tell the caller that a fraud flag was set. They say the request needs another review and give the ticket number. Finance and the fraud desk own the flag after that. Agents do not clear the flag themselves.
