# Porting a number out

A port-out request is completed in 1 business day after the account PIN is verified. Agents confirm the PIN with the customer and then release the number. Agents do not refuse a port-out because of a retention offer. An unpaid final balance does not block the port. The line stops working when the other carrier completes the port, and the agent tells the customer that before releasing it.
