# Identity verification

Before an agent discusses charges, the customer must pass one of these checks: the account PIN, or the one-time text code plus the last four digits of the account number. Callers who fail both are given the public reset steps and the call ends. Agents do not accept a photo of an identity document over chat. A failed check is noted on the ticket without storing the PIN the caller tried.
