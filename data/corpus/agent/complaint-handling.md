# Complaint handling

Agents log a complaint ticket when the customer says they want a record, even if the issue is solved on the call. The ticket includes the customer's own description in quotation marks and the agent's actions underneath. Agents do not close a complaint ticket on the same call unless the customer agrees. A complaint about billing is tagged billing. A complaint about a person is tagged conduct and is not coached on the same call.
