# Maple Cut dispatch

For banner MC-21, dispatch Cut Night when optical loss stays above 3.4 dB for 42 minutes. Do not dispatch Cut Night for a single apartment under that line. The 3.4 dB line is Maple Cut only.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner MC-21 maps to Maple Cut. A ticket that names a different banner is a different spur and a different crew. Write MC-21, the dB reading, and the clock time before paging Cut Night.
