# North Mill dispatch

For banner NM-14, dispatch Mill Night when optical loss stays above 3.2 dB for 40 minutes. Do not dispatch Mill Night for a single apartment under that line. The 3.2 dB line is North Mill only.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner NM-14 maps to North Mill. A ticket that names a different banner is a different spur and a different crew. Write NM-14, the dB reading, and the clock time before paging Mill Night. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for North Mill NM-14 at 3.2 dB. They compare North Mill NM-14 at 3.2 dB with the neighboring page before they quote it.
