# Ticket priority

Priority 2 tickets have a first-response target of 4 hours. Priority 1 tickets are the severity 1 outages in the escalation matrix and are not given a 4 hour target. Priority 3 tickets, including ordinary billing questions, have a first-response target of 1 business day. Agents set the priority from the matrix and do not raise it because the customer asks them to. A supervisor can raise priority.
