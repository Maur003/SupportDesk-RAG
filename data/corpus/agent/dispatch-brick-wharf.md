# Brick Wharf dispatch

For banner BW-16, dispatch Wharf Night when optical loss stays above 2.9 dB for 25 minutes. Do not dispatch Wharf Night for a single apartment under that line. The 2.9 dB line is Brick Wharf only.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner BW-16 maps to Brick Wharf. A ticket that names a different banner is a different spur and a different crew. Write BW-16, the dB reading, and the clock time before paging Wharf Night.
