# Agent outage credits

Agents may apply an outage credit of at most 3 days without finance approval. The outage must match an open banner or a closed banner whose duration was at least 12 hours. Agents do not apply a credit for planned maintenance that was announced at least 24 hours ahead. A request above 3 days is handed to finance with the banner id attached. The credit is days of recurring service, not cash.
