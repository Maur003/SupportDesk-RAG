# Agent outage credits

Agents may apply an outage credit of at most 3 days without finance approval. The outage must match an open banner or a closed banner whose duration was at least 12 hours. Agents do not apply a credit for planned maintenance that was announced at least 24 hours ahead. A request above 3 days is handed to finance with the banner id attached. The credit is days of recurring service, not cash.

## How the request is opened

A residential agent outage credit starts from an open banner or a closed banner whose duration met the line. The qualifying continuous gap is 12 hours, which is not the figure on the neighboring page. The size of the credit is at most 3 days. Planned work that was announced at least 24 hours ahead does not qualify on this page. Frontline staff may apply the credit without the ledger desk. Above that size, the ticket goes to the ledger desk with the banner id attached. Cash is not offered on this page. The neighboring page is the customer outage-credit page. That page uses a different size (one day, and only when the household asks) and a different audience. ## What does not qualify

A gap shorter than the figure on this page does not qualify. A single television app, a handset on wireless, or a gateway that was unplugged does not qualify. The credit is service days or a stated fraction of a day, posted on a later invoice. It is not a refund of a payment, and it is not the the customer outage-credit page rule.
