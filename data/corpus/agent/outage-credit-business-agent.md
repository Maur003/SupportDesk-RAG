# Agent outage credits for small business

A continuous outage of 8 hours or more on a small-business agent account is handled as at most 2 days. This is not the residential customer rule and it is not the residential agent rule.

## How the request is opened

A small-business agent outage credit starts from a business banner id. The qualifying continuous gap is 8 hours, which is not the figure on the neighboring page. The size of the credit is at most 2 days. Planned work that was announced at least 24 hours ahead does not qualify on this page. Frontline staff may apply at most 2 days on a business banner. A larger request goes to the ledger desk. Do not apply the residential 3 day cap on a business account. Cash is not offered on this page. Staff write the clock time and the ticket id on the note for small-business agent outage credit of at most 2 days. They compare small-business agent outage credit of at most 2 days with the neighboring page before they quote it. The neighboring page uses the same steps and a different figure. Quoting the neighboring figure on a small-business agent outage credit of at most 2 days note is a misread. The note stays on the account for 24 months. ## What does not qualify

A gap shorter than the figure on this page does not qualify. A single television app, a handset on wireless, or a gateway that was unplugged does not qualify. The credit is service days or a stated fraction of a day, posted on a later invoice. It is not a refund of a payment, and it is not the the residential agent outage page rule.
