# South Junction dispatch

For banner SJ-18, dispatch Junction Night when optical loss stays above 3.6 dB for 45 minutes. Do not dispatch Junction Night for a single apartment under that line. The 3.6 dB line is South Junction only.

## Recognizing the banner

Banner SJ-18 maps to South Junction. A ticket that names a different banner is a different spur and a different crew. Write SJ-18, the dB reading, and the clock time before paging Junction Night.
