# What a supervisor can approve

A supervisor can approve a second retention save in the same year and can raise ticket priority. A supervisor cannot approve a goodwill credit above the finance analyst limit. That request goes to finance. Supervisors can waive one missed-installation fee of 50 dollars per account per year. The waiver is noted with the supervisor's employee id.
