# Deceased account holder

When a caller reports that the account holder has died, the agent expresses condolences and stops collection language. The agent asks for the contact's name and a callback number, and opens a bereavement ticket. Service can stay on for 30 days while the estate decides. Agents do not require a document on the first call. Finance handles any refund of a credit balance after the bereavement ticket is open.
